Method

Transparent value formula

Utility = Σ(weight × normalized feature score)
Value density = Utility score ÷ price in ₹ lakh
Premium test = extra utility ÷ extra money spent

Benchmarks are broad caps, not opponent-relative scores. This avoids manufacturing a winner by moving the goalposts whenever you change bikes.

  • Performance combines top speed, peak power and acceleration when available.
  • Range uses claimed EV range or mileage for combustion bikes.
  • Charging rewards faster 0–80% charging when a source provides it.
  • Safety rewards ABS coverage and traction control where recorded.
  • Maintenance rewards lower routine ownership cost; when no credible annual-cost figure is available, the score stays neutral and the gap is shown as missing data.

Important: claimed range is not real-world range. Prices can vary by city, variant, offers and registration costs.
